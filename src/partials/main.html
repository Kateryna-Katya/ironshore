Ironshore

Echo Diver

ECHO-LOCATION

In the lightless depths of the Ironshore, your eyes are useless. Sound is your only canvas, and silence is the wall. Send a pulse to illuminate the void, mapping the world one echo at a time.

Idea

Every pulse is a fleeting heartbeat that sketches the forgotten geometry of the deep.

Echo Diver removes traditional visibility. Players move through space relying on echo pulses that briefly illuminate the surroundings. You'll find yourself getting into this weird rhythm - step forward, ping, wait to see what shows up, then figure out where to go next.

You can't rush this one - every step matters, and honestly, that's what makes it so absorbing.

Flow

Move. Pulse. Understand.

At the start, the space feels empty. A single pulse reveals fragments — shapes, distances, possible paths. With each pulse, players begin to understand the environment.

You're basically sketching out the world in your head, piece by piece, from these quick flashes of what's around you.

ACOUSTIC VISION

Play

There is no rush — awareness is the key.

Every movement in Ironshore is a calculated risk taken in total darkness. Success is not measured by speed, but by your ability to interpret the fleeting geometry of sound and maintain your bearing in the silent expanse.

  • Simple actions, thoughtful movement

  • Move through the environment slowly

  • Send out an echo pulse

  • Observe revealed shapes

  • Adjust direction based on new information

  • Continue deeper into the unknown
